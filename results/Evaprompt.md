# The prompt of LLMs for evaluation

## The Hallucination evaluation

You are an expert in the field of organic photovoltaics with deep knowledge of materials science, device physics, and the current literature. Please evaluate the following two responses. Score them on **Factuality Hallucination** (generation of content that is factually incorrect or unsupported by real-world information, including made-up statistics, dates, names, events, or scientific facts) and **Faithfulness Hallucination** (generation of content that deviates from or contradicts the provided source material or prompt, even if the content is factually correct in isolation). Provide a score out of 10 for each dimension.

## The completeness, scientific rigor, and temporal relevance evaluation

You are an expert in the field of organic photovoltaics with deep knowledge of materials science, device physics, and the current literature. Please evaluate the following response based on the three criteria:

- **Precision & Logical Soundness**: This dimension assesses the logical coherence of its presentation.
- **Completeness & Technical Depth**: This dimension evaluates the extent to which the response covers all necessary aspects of the prompt and demonstrates a sound understanding of underlying technical concepts.
- **Temporal Relevance**: This dimension assesses whether the information presented in the response is current and up-to-date.

Provide a score out of 10 for each dimension, along with a brief justification for each score, and give the average scores as the following scores.
